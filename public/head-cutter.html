Where does the head end?

The generator draws whole people, because a headless figure is an unnatural thing to ask for and comes back wrong. Drag a box round each head. The bottom centre of the box — the green dot — is the neck anchor, and it is the real output: it is what puts a head back on a body in the same place across every pose, so heads do not jump about as somebody walks. Drag again to redo one. Then copy the JSON and run python3 docs/tools/cut_heads.py cuts.json.
