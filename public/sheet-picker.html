Which one got it right?

The generator makes a sheet of candidates. Phase one: drag a box round the cell that got the pose right. Phase two: on that cell, click the six landmarks in order — neck left, neck right, hand left, hand right, foot left, foot right. Those six points are what lets the sprite land on a real sheet at the right size and place. Name the pose, then move to the next sheet. Copy the JSON and run python3 docs/tools/cut_cells.py picks.json.

Nothing leaves this browser. Sheets are read from your disk and never uploaded.
